# Deployment status and remaining work

The application now has a persistent AI adoption workflow, authentication, migrations, independent review, evidence freshness checks, exports, a Gunicorn container and automated validation.

The maintained deployment instructions are in [docs/OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md). The product review, delivered scope and remaining production work are in [docs/REVIEW_AND_ROADMAP.md](docs/REVIEW_AND_ROADMAP.md).

This release supports a single small organizational workspace. It does not claim isolated multitenancy, SSO/MFA, high availability, complete legal coverage or certification. Broader rollout should follow the acceptance criteria in the roadmap.
